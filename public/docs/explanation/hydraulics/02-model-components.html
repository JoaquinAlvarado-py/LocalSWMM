Node data ​

Each node carries static geometric properties and dynamic state (NodeData.hpp):

  • zinvz_{\mathrm{inv}}zinv​ — invert elevation (ft).
  • dfulld_{\mathrm{full}}dfull​ — full depth (depth to the rim / top of storage).
  • dsurd_{\mathrm{sur}}dsur​ — maximum allowed surcharge depth above full depth; the flooding limit is zinv+dfull+dsurz_{\mathrm{inv}} + d_{\mathrm{full}} + d_{\mathrm{sur}}zinv​+dfull​+dsur​.
  • ApondA_{\mathrm{pond}}Apond​ — ponded surface area used when the node floods above its rim and ponding is enabled.
  • zcrownz_{\mathrm{crown}}zcrown​ — elevation of the crown of the highest connecting conduit; the surcharge threshold is ycrown=zcrown−zinvy_{\mathrm{crown}} = z_{\mathrm{crown}} - z_{\mathrm{inv}}ycrown​=zcrown​−zinv​.

  • VfullV_{\mathrm{full}}Vfull​ — volume at full depth.

  • ddd — water depth above the invert (ft);
  • H=zinv+dH = z_{\mathrm{inv}} + dH=zinv​+d — hydraulic head (ft);
  • VVV — stored volume (ft3^33);
  • QlatQ_{\mathrm{lat}}Qlat​ — total lateral inflow (ft3^33/s);
  • QinQ_{\mathrm{in}}Qin​, QoutQ_{\mathrm{out}}Qout​ — total inflow and outflow;
  • QovflQ_{\mathrm{ovfl}}Qovfl​ — overflow/flooding rate;
  • QlossQ_{\mathrm{loss}}Qloss​ — evaporation + seepage/exfiltration loss rate;
  • ΔQnett−1\Delta Q_{\mathrm{net}}^{t-1}ΔQnett−1​ — net inflow Qin−QoutQ_{\mathrm{in}} - Q_{\mathrm{out}}Qin​−Qout​ of the previous step (used for trapezoidal averaging);
