Node Continuity ​

Node continuity, flooding, and surcharge ​

Net flow and volume change ​

At each node, per Picard iteration (DynamicWave.cpp, function setNodeDepth), the net flow and trapezoidal volume change are:

ΔQ=Qin−Qout,ΔV=12(ΔQnett−1+ΔQ)Δt,

where ΔQnett−1 is the net flow of the previous step (trapezoidal/Crank–Nicolson averaging).

Free-surface update (EXPLICIT continuity) ​

For a node that is not surcharged, the depth update is

Δy=ΔVAS,yt+Δt=yt+Δy,

where the assembly surface area is floored by the minimum surface area,

AS=max(ASN+∑ASL, Amin),Amin=12.566 ft2,

the default (overrideable via the MIN_SURFAREA option). The minimum area is a purely computational device; it does not add volume.

Surcharge (EXTRAN) update ​

A node is surcharged when all conduits connected to it are full or its head exceeds the crown of its highest conduit (y>ycrown), and it is not a storage or outfall node and is not ponding. Under EXTRAN the continuity equation becomes ∑Q=0, enforced through a perturbation (Newton/Hardy Cross) form. The surcharged depth update is

Δy=αΔQdenom,yt+Δt=yt+Δy,

with the terminal-node correction α=0.6 for upstream terminal nodes (only outflow links, deg<0) and 1 otherwise, and a crown-proximity blend for a smooth transition into/out of surcharge:

denom=∑dQdH+(ASoldΔt−∑dQdH)e−15f,f=yt−ycrownycrown,

applied while yt<1.25ycrown. The head is kept at or above the crown (y≥ycrown−FUDGE). The ∑dQ/dH accumulated at each node is the sum of the per-link gradients (Eq. eq:dqdh, see The link momentum kernel); note the engine accumulates ∑dQ/dH as a positive magnitude with dQnet/dH=−∑dQ/dH (a head rise increases net outflow).

Semi-implicit node continuity ​

The NODE_CONTINUITY option (default EXPLICIT) can select the unified semi-implicit formulation, a single smooth expression valid for both free-surface and surcharged states. Linearizing the net flow about the current head estimate with the flow gradients, ∑Qt+Δt≈∑Q+∑(dQ/dH)ΔH, and substituting into the trapezoidal head update yields

Δy=ΔVAS+Δt2∑dQdH,yt+Δt=yt+Δy,

with the denominator floored by Amin. The flow-gradient term damps the update (a head rise that increases net outflow reduces the net fill), and the expression is C1-smooth through the crown, which is what makes it compatible with Anderson acceleration.

Flooding and ponding ​

The maximum non-flooded depth is ymax=dfull+dsur (for non-ponding nodes; ponding nodes are never capped). If the candidate depth exceeds ymax:

  • Non-ponding: the head is capped at ymax and the excess is lost as overflow,

    Qovfl=ΔVΔt,V=Vfull;

  • Ponding (with ALLOW_PONDING and a positive ponded area): the ponded node acts as a constant-area storage node above the rim,

    V=max(Vold+ΔV, Vfull),Qovfl=V−max(Vold, Vfull)Δt,

    and the head is free to rise above the rim.

A ponded node is not allowed to drop much below full depth once ponded (y≥dfull−FUDGE).
