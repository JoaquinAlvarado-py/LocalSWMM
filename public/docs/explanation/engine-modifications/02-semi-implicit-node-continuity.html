Semi-implicit node continuity (NODE_CONTINUITY) ​

Motivation ​

In classical SWMM, the head update at a node uses two separate branches:

  • Free surface: Δy=ΔV/AS\Delta y = \Delta V / A_SΔy=ΔV/AS​, with ASA_SAS​ the surface area of the node assembly;
  • Surcharged (EXTRAN): Δy=α ΔQ/denom\Delta y = \alpha\,\Delta Q / \mathrm{denom}Δy=αΔQ/denom, where the denominator incorporates the gradient ∑dQ/dH\sum dQ/dH∑dQ/dH.

The switch between the two branches occurs exactly at the crown elevation of the highest conduit connected to the node. The head update operator is, therefore, discontinuous at the crown: a node entering or leaving the surcharged condition changes formula from one step to the next. This discontinuity is a source of oscillations and extra iterations in networks that repeatedly pass through the crown (very common in combined sewer networks).

The unified formulation ​

The NODE_CONTINUITY SEMI_IMPLICIT extension replaces the two branches with a single smooth expression. The idea is to recognize that the flows entering the continuity equation update depend on the very head being solved. Linearizing the net flow around the current estimate with the flow gradients:

∑Qt+Δt≈∑Q+∑dQdH ΔH,\sum Q^{t+\Delta t} \approx \sum Q + \sum\frac{dQ}{dH}\,\Delta H, ∑Qt+Δt≈∑Q+∑dHdQ​ΔH,

and substituting into the trapezoidal head update,

ΔV=Δt2(∑Qt+∑Qt+Δt),\Delta V = \tfrac{\Delta t}{2}\left(\sum Q^{t} + \sum Q^{t+\Delta t}\right), ΔV=2Δt​(∑Qt+∑Qt+Δt),

one obtains the unified expression that the engine implements in setNodeDepth (DynamicWave.cpp):

Δy=ΔVAS+Δt2∑dQdH,yt+Δt=yt+Δy,\Delta y = \frac{\Delta V}{A_S + \tfrac{\Delta t}{2}\sum \frac{dQ}{dH}}, \qquad y^{t+\Delta t} = y^{t} + \Delta y, Δy=AS​+2Δt​∑dHdQ​ΔV​,yt+Δt=yt+Δy,

with the denominator floored by the minimum area Amin⁡A_{\min}Amin​.

Interpretation and sign convention ​

The engine accumulates ∑dQ/dH\sum dQ/dH∑dQ/dH as a positive magnitude, with dQnet/dH=−∑dQ/dHdQ_{\mathrm{net}}/dH = -\sum dQ/dHdQnet​/dH=−∑dQ/dH (a head rise increases the net outflow). Under that convention, the term Δt2∑dQ/dH\tfrac{\Delta t}{2}\sum dQ/dH2Δt​∑dQ/dH in the denominator is positive and damps the update: a head rise that increases the net outflow reduces the net filling. The behavior is as expected in both regimes:

  • when the surface area dominates the denominator, the expression collapses to the classical free-surface formula Δy=ΔV/AS\Delta y = \Delta V/A_SΔy=ΔV/AS​;
  • when the node approaches and crosses the crown, the flow-gradient term takes over the role that the EXTRAN surcharge formula plays in the explicit formulation, without switching branch.

This is a correction of the sign ambiguity in the official SWMM manual: the engine was corrected to use the plus sign (DynamicWave.cpp:3374, with the derivation comment ΔH=ΔV/(AS+0.5 Δt sumdqdh)\Delta H = \Delta V/(A_S + 0.5\,\Delta t\,\mathrm{sumdqdh})ΔH=ΔV/(AS​+0.5Δtsumdqdh) for sumdqdh>0\mathrm{sumdqdh} > 0sumdqdh>0).

Advantages ​

Semi-implicit node continuity:

  • eliminates the crown branch, so the operator is C1C^1C1-smooth across the free-surface ↔\leftrightarrow↔ surcharged transition;
  • is the recommended setting for models with virtual junctions (Section Virtual junctions), because the momentum coupling through the node requires smoothness;
  • is the natural configuration to combine with Anderson acceleration (Section Anderson acceleration): under EXPLICIT, surcharged nodes are excluded from the acceleration, whereas under SEMI_IMPLICIT they remain eligible.
